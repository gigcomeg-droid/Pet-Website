THE COVER

GIG-Egypt Pet Insurance protects the pets your clients own. Covers, limits and exclusions are set out in the pet insurance policy schedule.

2

THE BENEFIT FOR YOUR CLIENT

Pet owners who use your promo code receive a 5% discount on their pet insurance premium — an immediate saving at the point of care.

3

HOW IT WORKS

Every insured veterinarian is issued a unique promo code. The client enters it when buying the policy and the 5% discount is applied at inception.

Pet insurance is a separate policy issued to the pet owner and is not part of the veterinarian’s package.
